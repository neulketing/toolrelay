# Security

Report a vulnerability privately: open this repo's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**.
Please do not open a public issue for it.

In scope: leaking provider keys or caller keys, spending past a key's balance or `X-Max-Cost`,
reaching paid tools without a key, and bypassing the admin token.

We reply within 3 business days and credit you in the release notes unless you ask us not to.
